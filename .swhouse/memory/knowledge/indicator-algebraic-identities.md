topic: Exact algebraic identities between common indicators, used to judge redundancy in a linear score
---

Notation: `C` close, `e` EMA of `C` with coefficient `c` seeded at the first value, `d = ln(C / e)` (log distance from the EMA), `EMA0(x; c)` an EMA of `x` seeded at zero, `A = EMA0(dC; c)`, `B = EMA0(abs(dC); c)`. Each identity was computed by the Critic (Step 5), regenerated by the Builder's scripts and proved again by hand and in Python by the Scientist (Step 9); residuals at rounding level (1e-15 to 1e-17).

| # | Identity | Consequence for a linear score |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | `ln(e_fast / e_slow) = d_slow - d_fast` | log MACD is an exact combination of two distances **only when the speeds coincide**; with other speeds (MACD 12/26 against distances at 10, 20, 50) R² 0.9968: approximation, not identity |
| I2 | `e[t]/e[t-1] - 1 = c * (C[t]/e[t-1] - 1)`; with `u = C[t]/e[t-1] - 1`, `d[t] = ln(1+u) - ln(1+c*u)` | the EMA slope is a monotone function of `d` **of the same bar** (correlation 0.9995 with `d[t]`, about 0.90 with `d[t-1]`), not "`d` one day late" |
| I3 | `2 * RSI/100 - 1 = A / B` (Wilder averages, same seeding as TA-Lib) | centred RSI is a ratio of two EMAs; Kaufman's signed efficiency is only a relative (correlation 0.89) |
| I4 | `%B = 0.5 + z / (2k)`, `z = (C - SMA) / sigma` of the prices in the window (population sigma) | %B and the Bollinger z are the same input; with a z normalised by return volatility (N2) only close (correlation about 0.93) |
| I5 | `A = c / (1 - c) * (C - e)` | `rsi_c = c/(1-c) * (C - e) / B`: centred RSI is the distance **in euro** from the EMA divided by the mean absolute change, times the constant `c/(1-c)`; bounded in [-1, 1] because `abs(A) <= B` |

Proof of I5: with `D = C - e` and `D[1] = 0`, the EMA recursion gives `D[t] = (1-c)(D[t-1] + dC[t])`; `X = c/(1-c) * D` then satisfies `X[t] = (1-c) X[t-1] + c dC[t]`, `X[1] = 0`, the recursion of `A`. I5 has no external source: it is a derivation of the cycle 011 catalog. Its first wording (Step 7) was wrong, see [[2026-10-18-identity-claims-imprecise]].

**Method that goes with them** (operator decision Q3, [[2026-10-18-indicator-catalog-constraints]]): redundancy is judged first by these identities, then on a synthetic random walk (redundancy "by construction", not of the market: distance-MACD 0.938 on ENI and 0.937 on a random walk), and only last on ENI as a declared boundary check. Correlations on ENI are unstable between stretches (distance-RSI 0.79 on the first 40 %, 0.93 on the rest) and choosing inputs on them is selection on the evaluation data (R6).
